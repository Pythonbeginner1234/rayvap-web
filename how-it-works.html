How It Works

RVAP structures each investment around an agreed improvement, project cost and baseline rent.

RVAP and the property owner identify an improvement expected to increase the rental value of an apartment, and agree on the baseline rent against which the resulting uplift will be measured.

Realize the value

If requested, RVAP coordinates the re-leasing of the improved apartment through a licensed real estate broker or otherwise assists the property owner in realizing the increased rental value created by the improvement.

CAPITAL RECOUPMENT
4.

Recoup the investment

RVAP receives an agreed portion of the incremental rental income until its invested capital has been recovered.

OWNER + RVAP
SHARE
INCREMENTAL VALUE
5.

Share the value created

Following recoupment, RVAP and the property owner share the incremental rental income for the remainder of the agreed term.

The structure allows owners to make revenue-enhancing improvements without an upfront capital expenditure while retaining a portion of the resulting rental upside. RVAP assumes the initial capital commitment and execution responsibility and earns its return from the value created.
